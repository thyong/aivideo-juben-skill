# Fixed visual specification

Use the following text verbatim as every shot's `画面风格` value. Do not add time-specific lighting here.

This fixed block defines overall spatial hierarchy and atmospheric depth, not optical focus. Decide shallow, medium-shallow, medium, or medium-deep depth of field separately for every shot and write it in `镜头`; never append one global depth-of-field phrase to this block.

> 100%写实的高端电影级视觉效果，真实摄影质感与精致童话美学融合，超写实CG渲染，但最终画面必须像在真实场景中使用专业电影摄影机拍摄的照片，不呈现卡通、塑料玩具或插画质感。整体采用海洋治愈系视觉体系，以明亮天空蓝、清澈海水蓝、淡冰蓝和纯净奶油白为核心色，蓝色为绝对主色，奶油粉仅作为少量辅助色和视觉点缀；糖果色电影配色，色彩饱满但柔和不刺眼，避免全画面被单一深蓝、粉色、橙黄色或棕色覆盖。顶级电影级光影，低对比、高动态范围HDR，柔和高光，暗部通透并保留丰富细节，无死黑阴影。主体毛发保持自然固有颜色，白色毛发通透柔亮，不被蓝色环境光染成灰蓝色；蓝色环境光仅用于轻微边缘轮廓和空间反射。画面具有轻微高级胶片色彩倾向和商业广告级色彩分级，暖色自然、蓝色清澈，避免复古泛黄和灰暗滤镜。画面明亮、柔和、清新、梦幻，具有高端海边度假的温暖治愈氛围和甜美童话感。空气清澈通透，毛发、织物、木材、石材、金属、玻璃、水体和湿润地面呈现真实、细腻、可触摸的材质。主体角色是绝对视觉中心，脸部、眼睛和关键动作清晰突出。场景采用真实可信的建筑、道路、家具和自然环境，不使用夸张卡通布景；减少与剧情无关的杂乱物体，但保留完整空间结构和清晰的前景、中景、远景层次。画面具有自然纵深和空气透视，远景环境可辨识，但对比度与饱和度略低于主体。电影剧照质感，商业广告级完成度，精致但不虚假，梦幻但保持真实。

## Palette guardrail

Treat these as targets, not reasons to recolor functional props:

- sky blue, ocean blue, ice blue, cream white: 60–75% combined;
- pale gold and cream pink accents: 10–25% combined;
- navy, black, dark brown: normally no more than 15%;
- preserve recognizable prop colors when story clarity requires them.

## Lighting adapters

Write an adapter into `光线` according to the actual story time.

### Day

Use soft warm natural key light plus bright pale-blue sky/sea fill. Keep facial exposure clear, shadows soft, highlights controlled, and air transparent.

### Dusk

Use pale gold or champagne side/back light plus large-area blue frontal fill. Balance indoor/outdoor exposure. Avoid orange dominance, brown wash, and silhouette.

### Night

Use neutral-white key light on faces and upper bodies plus ice-blue environmental fill and restrained warm-white practical lights. Keep the night bright and readable without turning it into daylight, cyberpunk, or suspense lighting.

### Interior

Use neutral warm-white key light plus plausible blue window/door fill. Let light-colored walls and floors provide bounce. Preserve exterior time and balance window highlights with interior shadows.
